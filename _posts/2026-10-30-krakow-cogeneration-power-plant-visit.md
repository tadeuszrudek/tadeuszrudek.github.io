⚡ Today, together with the students of **Technology and Transformation** at the **Institute of Sociology, Jagiellonian University**, we had a great opportunity to visit the **cogeneration power plant in Kraków** 🏭, which supplies heat and electricity to our city.

During the visit we saw the heart of the plant:

- 🖥️ the **power plant control room**,
- 🔥 the **boiler houses**,
- 🔌 the **stations transmitting energy to the grid**,
- 💨 the **flue gas and water treatment installations**.

With our excellent guide, an engineer working at the plant, we also talked about the changes the plant is going through right now, including the **new gas-fired units under construction** and the broader path towards **decarbonisation** 🌱. Seeing these infrastructures up close made it much easier to discuss how large-scale energy transitions actually unfold, on the ground and in the everyday life of a city.

🙏 Many thanks to our guide and to the whole team for hosting us!

Tadeusz Józef Rudek
