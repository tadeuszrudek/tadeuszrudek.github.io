We are delighted to announce the opening of a new academic year of **CI Seminars LAB**, marking a special milestone: the **5th anniversary of Capturing Invisible LAB**!

We will kick off this anniversary edition on **Thursday, 15 October 2026**, with **Dr Makoto Takahashi**, Executive Director of The McQuillan Institute for Science, Technology, and the Human Future, University of Cambridge.

Dr Takahashi will deliver a fascinating talk entitled **"Three Lions and a Unionist: Two Tours of the Fukushima Zone."**

📅 15 October 2026 | 15:00–16:00 CEST  
💻 Online

And this is just the beginning! Our anniversary programme will feature inspiring international scholars, including **Prof. Magdalena Kuchler** (Uppsala University), who will join us on **12 November** to explore the topographies of power in sociotechnical imaginaries.

Join us as we celebrate five years of interdisciplinary dialogue, critical inquiry, and collective exploration of science, technology, and society!

Everyone is warmly welcome!
