Today, **26 November**, we're hosting the **PANTHEON EU Knowledge Arena** online, from **15:00 to 16:30 CET**.

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/pantheon-eu-knowledge-arena-2026.png" class="img-fluid d-block mx-auto my-4" style="max-width: 520px;" alt="Poster for the PANTHEON EU Knowledge Arena, 26 November 2026, 15:00–16:30 CET online, with an illustration of people discussing an energy scenario chart, and the next arena in January 2027 at Peking University, Beijing">

At last year's [scoping workshops]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2026-04-01-pantheon-scoping-workshops %}), we asked participants what they wanted everyday life to look like after decarbonisation. **Affordable energy** was one of their priorities. It raises a question worth keeping in mind when we look at the results: **even if energy becomes more affordable on average, who might still struggle to pay the bill?**

Today, our team at **Jagiellonian University** is bringing workshop participants and modellers from **Universidad de Valladolid** and **Universidad de Zaragoza** back together. Our modelling colleagues, **Jaime Nieto Vega** and **Cristina Sarasa**, will show how they translated workshop priorities into scenarios and share the first results from **WILIAM**, our integrated assessment model. Then we'll ask participants:

- Do you recognise what mattered to you?
- What have we missed?
- What would you change?

Whether you took part last year or are joining us for the first time, we'd be glad to hear your perspective.

🇨🇳 In **January 2027**, we'll continue the conversation in person at **Peking University in Beijing**, where **Arkadiusz Szlaga (史建辉)** and **Tadeusz Józef Rudek** will organise a PANTHEON Knowledge Arena with our Chinese partners. Stay tuned!
