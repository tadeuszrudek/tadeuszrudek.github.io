After my PhD, I found myself looking for a new research path. This is a tough and often stressful moment: trying to find a direction that stays close to your own interests, while also bringing possible benefits to wider society.

This week, I had the opportunity to present my idea for a long-term project on **Assemblages of the Future and Sea Societies**.

![Presenting Sea Societies at the Science Democracy Network]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/zdj-1.png)

I am very grateful to the **Science Democracy Network** for listening to my ideas for future work and for bringing so many thoughtful comments, questions, and perspectives to consider.
