## Power, materiality and collective agency in sociotechnical imaginaries

Magdalena Kuchler's research has made a significant contribution to advancing and critically rethinking the concept of **sociotechnical imaginaries**. Her work has been particularly influential in bringing questions of **power, materiality and collective agency** into this field.

She has examined how **Poland's coal-dependent futures** are sustained and asked what we actually mean by the "collective" in **collective imaginaries**. Her research challenges us to look beyond visions of desirable futures. It investigates the political and material conditions that make some futures possible while marginalising others.

## Bounded imaginaries and just energy transitions in Upper Silesia

In her most recent work, Kuchler turns to local, **"bounded" imaginaries**. These emerge from the lived experience of **mining communities in Upper Silesia** and can serve as a resource for **socially just energy transitions**.
